Debra Finerman

Your book, in your language, wherever you are in the world.

American, a graduate in Political Science from UCLA with a Master of Art from Christie’s, Debra Finerman heads our International Department. A former journalist for The Hollywood Reporter (monthly edition), Capital Style and Beverly Hills Magazine, she is the author of Mademoiselle Victorine (Random House · Three Rivers Press, 2007), a novel about Manet’s muse translated into six languages, and co-author with Patrice de Moncan of Histoire Amoureuse du Vin.
