Your book about your firm.

Your history, your unique business culture and perspective.

More than 250 books.

Since 1987, Les Éditions du Mécène has created and published books for private enterprises and the general public. More than 250 books have been created for some of the most important French and international companies, as well as for numerous prestigious cultural institutions.

Discover our exceptional references
Each book we create merits the title « a work of art ».

We take particular care to provide the highest quality in writing, in iconography, and in presentation. Each book we create merits the title « a work of art ».

Your company’s book, if you so desire, can be offered for sale in bookshops through our book distribution network.

Three possible models of participation.

01

Your own book

We write and publish a unique book which describes your history, culture, personnel, activities.

02

Sponsorship

You sponsor the creation by us of a book on any subject of your choice.

03

Personalisation

You choose a book already in our collection and personalize it with your logo and a preface by your Chairman of the Board.

To be offered to your clients and partners, or as gifts to mark special announcements by your company, end of the year holiday parties, campaign launches, P.R. initiatives, etc.

Personalising our existing books.

The method we use to personalize our existing books to your purposes: your logo and brand printed on the book jacket, plus a text from your President or person of your choice as a Preface to the book. The book becomes your own book, expressing the unique image and culture of your business.
